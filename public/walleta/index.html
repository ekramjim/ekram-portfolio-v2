Common questions

How do I sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, on each device. Walleta syncs through your private iCloud database automatically. There's nothing to set up in the app.

What does “free to plan” mean?

It’s the money in a fund that isn’t already set aside for an upcoming expense you’ve forecast. If a fund shows a shortfall, its forecasts add up to more than it holds.

Why isn’t an expected saving counted yet?

Expected savings, like a paycheck that hasn’t arrived, only count once you mark them received. In the fund, swipe the entry and tap Received.

How do I delete all my data?

Open Settings in Walleta and choose Erase All Data. This removes it from every device signed in to your iCloud account.
