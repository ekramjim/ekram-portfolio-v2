Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Walleta doesn’t collect any personal data.

Your data

The funds, savings and expenses you enter are stored on your device. If you’re signed in to iCloud, they’re also stored in your private iCloud database so they sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac. That data is held by Apple under your iCloud account and can’t be accessed by the developer.

No tracking

Walleta has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can delete all your data at any time in Walleta’s Settings → Erase All Data, or by deleting the app and removing its data from iCloud in your device’s iCloud settings.
